{# Settings → Feeders #}

Receiver & feeders

Feeders are optional. They share this SkyTrack's ADS-B data with third parties (FlightRadar24, FlightAware) in exchange for their services. A tile reading not installed just means the corresponding package isn't on this Pi yet — it is not an error. SkyTrack itself works whether or not any feeder is installed.

        If you share ADS-B data with FlightRadar24, PiAware, or FlightAware,
        paste your IDs here. These are stored encrypted in
        /var/lib/skytrack/auth.json and never committed to git.
        Saving a value here does not install the feeder package —
        the credentials just sit idle until you install it on the Pi.
