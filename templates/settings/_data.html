Storage

SkyTrack records every aircraft it sees to a local SQLite database. This card controls how much history is kept and how aggressively the database is compacted.

Hard limit on stored sightings, independent of age. The oldest rows get dropped first when the cap is exceeded. Prevents runaway growth on unusually busy days.

After the nightly prune, SkyTrack rewrites the database file to reclaim the disk space freed by deleted rows. Takes a few seconds; leave on unless you have a reason not to.
