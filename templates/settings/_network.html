Connectivity

How this SkyTrack reaches the internet and how operators reach it. Three independent roles share the same Pi: Cellular (optional modem, outbound only), Wi-Fi client (join a nearby network), and the built-in SkyTrack-Portal hotspot (operators connect directly to the device). Wi-Fi client and hotspot share one radio, so only one of them can be active at a time.

SkyTrack-Portal hotspot

The private Wi-Fi that operators connect to for first-boot setup, admin access, and offline use. Give this SSID and password to anyone who needs to reach the device without going through your office Wi-Fi.

Passwords are automatically generated in the easy-to-type form Word-Word-NN (for example Alpha-Bravo-47) so they're easy to read aloud and hard to mistype.

Set up from SSH on this device. This is the recommended method — the device creates and manages the tunnel directly with your Cloudflare account. 

sudo bash /opt/skytrack/scripts/setup_tunnel.sh skytrack-baycity skytrack-baycity.yourdomain.com

You'll be given a URL to open in any browser to authorize the device with your Cloudflare account. The script creates the tunnel, sets up DNS, and starts the service automatically.

Alternative: install with a dashboard token

From Cloudflare Zero Trust → Networks → Tunnels → Create → Cloudflared.
