{# Settings → Access #}

Admin PIN

The Admin PIN is the only credential needed to unlock Settings, Network, Integrations, Updates, and Logs. There is no username, no password — just 4 to 8 digits you'll remember. Change it here any time.

Sign-in session & lockout

Controls how long you stay signed in after entering your PIN, and how many wrong PIN attempts trigger a short automatic lockout. {% if not is_super %}These settings can only be changed by a super admin.{% endif %}
