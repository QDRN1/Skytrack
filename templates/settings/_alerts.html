{# Settings → Alerts #}

Alerts — master switch

SkyTrack has two independent categories of alerts: Flight alerts (noteworthy aircraft overhead) and Device alerts (the Pi itself is in trouble). Turn the whole system off here, or tune each category below.

When off, nothing below fires — not even device warnings.

{# ---------------- Device alerts ---------------- #}

Device alerts

Warn when something is wrong with the SkyTrack hardware or its uplinks.

Fires if the receiver stops seeing any aircraft for this long. 0 disables. A good early warning for a dead antenna or a stuck dump1090 service.

Warn once this much of the AeroAPI monthly spend has been used.

{# ---------------- Delivery ---------------- #}

How to notify

Where alerts actually show up. Sound uses the on-board buzzer (GPIO 18) — if your device doesn't have one wired, leave this on Toast.

Ignored if your device has no buzzer, or if delivery is set to toast-only.
